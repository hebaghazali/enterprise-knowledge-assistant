================================================================================
COMPANY OVERVIEW
================================================================================

NovaTech Solutions is a fictional mid-sized SaaS company with approximately 500 employees.
The company provides cloud-based analytics and workflow automation products to enterprise customers
across Europe, the Middle East, and North America.

Mission:
To empower businesses through reliable, secure, and intelligent software.

Core Values:
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership
- Continuous Learning
- Simplicity
- Integrity
- Collaboration

Headquarters: London, United Kingdom
Regional Offices: Cairo, Berlin, Toronto


================================================================================
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
================================================================================

Employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, respect colleagues,
protect confidential information, and adhere to company policies.

Standard workweek:
- Monday to Friday
- 40 hours per week

Core hours:
- 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time

Employees should keep calendars updated and communicate planned absences.


================================================================================
REMOTE WORK POLICY
================================================================================

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Additional remote days may be approved by managers for:
- Health circumstances
- Travel constraints
- Focus work periods

Employees must:
- Attend mandatory meetings
- Remain reachable during core hours
- Maintain secure working environments

Equipment remains company property.


================================================================================
TIME OFF POLICY
================================================================================

Annual Leave:
- 25 business days annually.

Sick Leave:
- Up to 10 paid sick days annually.
- Medical documentation may be required after three consecutive days.

Parental Leave:
- Primary caregivers: 16 weeks paid leave.
- Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks paid leave.


================================================================================
SECURITY POLICY
================================================================================

Passwords must use approved password managers.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for:
- Email accounts
- VPN access
- Production systems
- Cloud providers

Sharing credentials is prohibited.

Security incidents must be reported within one hour of discovery.


================================================================================
ENGINEERING STANDARDS
================================================================================

All production code requires peer review.

Requirements:
- Automated tests
- Clear naming conventions
- Documentation updates
- Monitoring considerations

High-risk changes require two approvals:
- Database migrations
- Authentication changes
- Payment processing logic
- Infrastructure modifications


================================================================================
DEPLOYMENT RUNBOOK
================================================================================

Production deployments occur Tuesday and Thursday.

Deployment checklist:
1. Verify tests pass.
2. Confirm rollback strategy.
3. Notify stakeholders.
4. Deploy using CI/CD.
5. Monitor logs and dashboards.
6. Validate business metrics.

Emergency hotfixes require Engineering Manager approval.


================================================================================
INCIDENT RESPONSE
================================================================================

Severity Levels:

SEV-1:
Complete service outage affecting critical customers.

SEV-2:
Major degradation affecting multiple customers.

SEV-3:
Partial degradation with workaround.

SEV-4:
Minor issue.

SEV-1 Escalation:
1. Notify Incident Commander.
2. Create incident bridge.
3. Engage on-call engineer.
4. Update status page every 30 minutes.
5. Document timeline.
6. Conduct postmortem within five business days.

Priority during incidents:
Mitigate first.
Investigate root cause after stabilization.


================================================================================
ON-CALL POLICY
================================================================================

Engineering teams participate in weekly rotations.

Expectations:
- Respond within 15 minutes.
- Acknowledge alerts.
- Escalate when necessary.

Escalation Path:
Primary Engineer → Secondary Engineer → Engineering Manager → Director of Engineering.


================================================================================
AWS INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================================

Core services include:
- ECS Fargate
- RDS PostgreSQL
- S3
- CloudWatch
- Lambda
- Step Functions
- Route 53
- IAM
- Secrets Manager

Production access requires approval and least-privilege permissions.


================================================================================
DATA RETENTION POLICY
================================================================================

Customer data:
Retained for seven years.

Audit logs:
Retained for three years.

Application logs:
Retained for ninety days.

Backup retention:
Thirty-five days.

Data deletion requests must comply with GDPR obligations.


================================================================================
CUSTOMER SUPPORT ESCALATIONS
================================================================================

Enterprise customers receive:
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Dedicated account managers
- Priority support

Escalation process:
Support → Technical Specialist → Engineering → Leadership.


================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS
================================================================================

ADR-001:
PostgreSQL selected for transactional workloads.

ADR-002:
Redis introduced for distributed caching.

ADR-003:
Parquet and DuckDB adopted for large-scale analytics.

ADR-004:
Background processing standardized using Celery workers.

ADR-005:
Containerized deployments standardized through Docker.
